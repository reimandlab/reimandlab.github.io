Somatic mutations accumulate in every cancer genome, yet only a few drive the disease. We develop statistical and machine-learning methods to find driver mutations in coding and non-coding DNA, and study the mutational processes, shaped by chromatin, carcinogens and therapies, that create the far more numerous passengers.
