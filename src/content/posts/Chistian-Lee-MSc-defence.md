Christian Lee successfully defended his master's thesis on regional mutation processes affecting regulatory elements in cancer genomes. Christian received a Master of Science (MSc) degree from the Department of Medical Biophysics, University of Toronto. Christian is the first author of our recent pan-cancer [study][paper_link] of regional mutagenesis and has taken leading roles in several collaborations during his studies. Congratulations!

[paper_link]: https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13059-021-02318-x
